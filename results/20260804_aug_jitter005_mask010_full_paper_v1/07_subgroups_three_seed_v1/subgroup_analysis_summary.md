# Subgroup analysis v1 summary

FCMAE(ECA) had a positive AUROC difference in 15/16 evaluated subgroups.
The largest AUROC difference was 0.0134 in Age/70-79; the smallest was -0.0022 in Race/Ethnicity/Asian.
The widest paired AUROC interval occurred in Race/Ethnicity/Asian (n=153, positive_n=70), consistent with greater sampling uncertainty in smaller or outcome-sparse groups.

## Age patterns

Age-specific AUROC and AUPRC are descriptive. AUPRC changes must be interpreted alongside prevalence; a higher AUPRC does not by itself prove stronger discrimination.
- 18-59: prevalence=0.316, FCMAE AUROC=0.861, FCMAE AUPRC=0.778.
- 60-69: prevalence=0.398, FCMAE AUROC=0.848, FCMAE AUPRC=0.808.
- 70-79: prevalence=0.501, FCMAE AUROC=0.815, FCMAE AUPRC=0.838.
- 80+: prevalence=0.644, FCMAE AUROC=0.789, FCMAE AUPRC=0.878.

## Statistical and clinical interpretation

9 subgroup AUROC comparisons met either a paired 95% interval excluding zero or AUROC q<0.05. Comparisons not meeting these criteria are described numerically rather than as significant improvements.
Overlapping subgroup confidence intervals do not establish fairness, and subgroup stability does not establish clinical equivalence.
This is a single-center fixed-test-set analysis and must not be presented as external validation or proof of external generalization.
Age and sex are both subgroup variables and FiLM inputs to the two matched models, so differences are descriptive model-behavior results rather than causal demographic effects.
